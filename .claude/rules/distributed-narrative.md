# Distributed-Narrative Methodology

Portfolio-wide pedagogy: **named recurring characters embody curricular concepts** — the cast IS the curriculum, not decorative mascot dressing. Grounded in Bruner narrative learning + Habgood intrinsic integration.

**Status**: 100% portfolio coverage (138/138 active apps) as of 2026-05-22. Every app ships a documented cast + site-visible "Meet the cast" section.

## When this rule applies

Any app that has `distributedNarrative: true` in `spark-anvil-site/src/data/apps.generated.ts` AND/OR a `Docs/HANDOFF_FROM_HUB_DISTRIBUTED_NARRATIVE_RETROFIT.md` (canonical 2026-06-11+) or `Docs/HANDOFF_FROM_LABSMITH_DISTRIBUTED_NARRATIVE_RETROFIT.md` (legacy pre-2026-06-11) in its repo. **All active portfolio apps meet this criterion** post-2026-05-22 — DN is the portfolio default.

## R-NARRATIVE-BETWEEN-NOT-DURING — narrative at session boundaries, not on the active learning loop (2026-07-07)

**Cast cameos, chapter beats, and any narrative surface MUST render at session boundaries (kit preview / results / the calm chapter reader) — NEVER overlaid on the active problem-solving loop.** During the practice moment itself the surface stays *schematic* (clean primitive representation; no busy scene, no chatter). Grounded in the distraction-hypothesis + seductive-details evidence above (P2): narrative on the critical path is extraneous cognitive load that measurably depresses learning, worst when the primitive is hard.
## R-GUARD-THE-RATIO — measure learning-relevant actions per session minute (2026-07-07)

**Every practice / game surface SHOULD instrument the game-to-learning ratio — learning-relevant actions (answers, selects, retrievals) per session minute vs. non-learning (decoration / animation / navigation) time — so decoration can never silently crowd out the content.** The Prodigy cautionary tale (educator estimates of ~5 min decorating : 2 min problem-solving at higher levels; the 2021 Fairplay/FTC complaint audited 16 membership ads vs 4 math problems in 19 min) was a *telemetry* finding — invisible without the ratio signal.

**AMENDMENT — the mentor ASKS, it never FLATTERS (the "seventh design law"; founder-approved 2026-09-06, Program #122 §11.5 / ADR-239).** The AI mentor/novice DEMONSTRATES, ASKS, and patiently scaffolds — **it never FLATTERS**: it never tells the child they are brilliant/smart/amazing, and it never uses person-praise or sycophancy to keep the child engaged. It may thank the child for a correction and show what it learned (the protégé loop), but the register is *warm + honest*, never flattering. This composes R-AI-MENTOR-INTEGRITY (answer-withholding) + the anti-extrinsic-reward / anti-shame canon + `R-YOUNGER-BAND-CONTENT-REGISTER` (process-praise not person-praise), and it **anticipates New York S.9051B**, which lists "flattery or sycophancy" as an *unsafe feature* for minors (a companion-law trend cheap to pre-empt). A mentor/novice surface (app template, teachable-cast, tutor) that flatters the child is a defect on the R-AI-MENTOR-INTEGRITY footing. Codified per Report No. 77 "A Diamond, Not a Ladder" §11.5 (the mentor template's "demonstrates, praises, and patiently scaffolds" → "demonstrates, **asks**, and patiently scaffolds; it never flatters"). Cross-ref: `spark-anvil-website.md` § R-SITE-AI-SAFETY-MOAT-MARKETING · `cognitive-accessibility.md` § R-COGNITIVE-ACCESSIBILITY (anti-shame, no person-praise).

## A cast-story chapter BODY must NOT print raw crisis-hotline NUMBERS — represent resource-AWARENESS in fable voice; the numbers live on the SITE SEL surface (R-CHAPTER-CRISIS-REGISTER; 2026-09-05)

**A kid-facing cast-story chapter BODY (the rendered prose — src `<Content/>` for single-beat, the `public/chapters/**` snapshot/beat-prose for multibeat) MUST NOT contain a raw crisis-hotline DIAL STRING or number-list — `988` / `741741` / any `1-800-…` line / `text HOME to …` / spelled-out digit strings / a "call/text these numbers" list of Childhelp·RAINN·NEDA·NCMEC·Crisis-Text-Line. Instead the body carries resource-AWARENESS in fable voice — *"there are people whose whole job is to listen, free and private, any hour, and a trusted grown-up can help you reach them"* (the medicquest/boundary.md base is the house pattern). The ACTUAL crisis footer with the real numbers/services lives on the SITE SEL surface (§ `spark-anvil-website.md` R-WEB-CLONE-SEL-CRISIS-FOOTER-SCOPE) — never baked into story prose a child reads/hears in a chapter. A dial string in a chapter body is a defect on the same footing as a condition-named-to-child (§ `cognitive-accessibility.md` R-COGNITIVE-ACCESSIBILITY); the child still gets the protective message (reach a trusted adult; there is always help), just not raw digits. Universal `911` is reframed as "a grown-up makes the emergency call" for consistency, not memorized digits.
